Privacy Policy for newSave

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

newSave is a client-side productivity tool designed to help students compile learning module content into offline PDF handouts. We respect your privacy: this extension operates entirely within your local browser and does not collect, track, or transmit your personal data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, student IDs, IP addresses, or account credentials.
  • No Authentication Data: The extension never intercepts, reads, or stores your university login passwords, session tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • No Content Telemetry: The course materials, text, and images extracted from your browser DOM are processed solely in local memory and are never uploaded to any external server or third-party service.

3. Data Processing and Storage

All DOM parsing, content extraction, and PDF generation occur entirely client-side (locally on your device). Once the PDF is compiled and saved to your device, the temporary memory buffer is released. No persistent databases, remote servers, or external cloud storage services are used.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

This extension does not integrate third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics), advertising networks, tracking pixels, or remote telemetry scripts.

5. Browser Permissions Used

  • activeTab / Host Permissions: Used exclusively to read the DOM structure of your active learning portal tab to extract module text and images for PDF conversion.
  • storage: Used strictly to maintain your local queue and legal consent agreement flag in local browser storage.

6. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub Issues.
